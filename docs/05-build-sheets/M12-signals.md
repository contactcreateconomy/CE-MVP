## 4. Domain Context
- **Terminology:**
  - **Signal** = a unit of **earned value**, reverse-attributed to a contribution from a real platform **outcome** (engagement + distribution + qualified CTA + conversion). Public. *(Was "system-internal, never surfaced" — evolved: now public, protected by the moat, not by hiding. DEC-M12-SIGNALS.)*
  - **Total Signals** = lifetime finalized Signals (persist; feed the permanent record + trust capacity). **Active Signals** = a 90-day weighted-decay window of finalized Signals (feed Might → Level; decay is the downgrade mechanism). **Pending Signals** = provisional, not yet settled. *(Two views of ONE ledger.)*
  - **Recognition** = a **backend** status score from local social wins (accepted answers, helpful comments, reviews, debate outcomes, participation). Produces **badges**, **Awards**, and **Podium** roles. **Never** feeds Signals/Might/trust/reach/ranking (firewall).
  - **Awards** = the **public** face of Recognition = a user's/Distribution's shelf + **count of earned badges** (level-milestone badges + recognition-role badges). Displayed as a count + shelf, not a raw number to grind.
  - **Legitimacy** = a hidden per-actor score in **[0,1]** (the moat) multiplied into **every** event's weight. Median honest actor ≈ **0.85**. Its *existence and purpose* are public; its *exact computation* is deliberately opaque (the one transparency exception).
  - **Distribution** = a creator's channel (single owner V1). **Reach** = the legitimacy-weighted count of verified, active, integrity-qualified **members** (people who deliberately joined).
  - **Might** = public creator power = `√(legitimacyWeightedReach × activeSignals)` — unbounded, geometric-mean (neither term can carry it alone).
  - **Level** = Might expressed as a **rank** on the cosmic ladder; a **percentile band** of Might among the eligible pool, frozen per Season. Downgradable.
  - **Signal Season** = a 1-year cycle; level thresholds are recomputed once at the boundary then **frozen** all year.
  - **Eligible competitive pool** = accounts that: own a Distribution · are a verified human · are ≥30 days old · are not staff/persona · have no active integrity hold · have ≥1 eligible contribution in 90 days · are above the participation floor. Percentiles are computed **only** over this pool.
- **Invariants:**
  - **INV-1 Firewall (evolved).** Recognition / Awards / Podium **never** feed Signals, Might, Reach, trust-tier, reaction-weight, feed ranking, or featuring. Signals/Might **never** derive from Recognition. The two live in different tables and never cross-reference.
  - **INV-2 The moat multiplies, silently.** Legitimacy ∈ [0,1] scales every event's contribution to Signals/Reach/trust. A suspected event **still ticks the visible number** but carries a **fractional weight** — there is **no feedback** telling an actor an event was discounted (removing the gaming gradient). Detection **neutralizes**, it does **not ban** (M13 owns any human-reviewed sanction).
  - **INV-3 Append-only ledger.** `signalLedger` is append-only; corrections are **reversal/clawback entries**, never edits. Displayed Signals = `max(Σ finalized, 0)`.
  - **INV-4 Value not actions.** Signals are awarded for **outcomes** (someone got value), never for the mere act of posting/reacting/logging-in/completing-a-profile (those may earn **Recognition**, never Signals).
  - **INV-5 Legitimacy-weighted reach (public count, internal factor).** The **public Reach number = the COUNT of verified/active/integrity-qualified members** (a clean integer). The **internal `reachFactor` that feeds Might is legitimacy-weighted** (Σ member.legitimacy): 1,000 bot members at 0.05 (= 50) rank below 100 real members at 0.85 (= 85). Fake members are filtered from the public count **and** down-weighted in the factor — worthless either way.
  - **INV-6 Levels change, badges don't.** Every level a Distribution ever reaches mints an **immutable** milestone badge in the Journal. A demotion changes the **live Level** only; the badge is **never** touched by inactivity or recalibration (only fraud/error/impersonation, via M13). Profile shows **"Peak"** and **"Now"** as two distinct fields, never merged.
  - **INV-7 Two-speed clock.** Might/percentile are computed **continuously** (backend). A user's **displayed Level** is committed on a **monthly** cadence so it never flickers from *other* people's activity. Season boundaries move the **thresholds**; the monthly commit moves the **person**.
  - **INV-8 Personas + staff = ZERO** in Signals, Reach, Might, Recognition, Podium, legitimacy graph, and every count — everywhere, always.
  - **INV-9 The moat is the one transparency exception.** WHAT (Signals, the basket, the ladder, the milestones) and WHY (design intent) are fully public and documented; **HOW legitimacy scores a specific interaction is deliberately not disclosed** — this is the only opaque surface in the product and is recorded as such.
  - **INV-10 Anti-suppression.** Because Level is relative, the attack surface includes **suppressing rivals** (moving the denominator), not just inflating yourself. Percentiles are computed over the **broadest eligible population**; leaving a rival's channel is log-scaled to a negligible reach effect; reports/hidden-negatives **never** touch a rival's legitimacy or Might until human moderation confirms.
  - **INV-11 Recipient Neutrality.** Unsolicited suspicious **inbound** activity never, by itself, lowers the recipient's Integrity Confidence / finalized Signals / Reach / Might; risk attaches to the **source actors + their cluster**, not the target; a target penalty requires **independent evidence of target involvement** (control / solicitation / payment / reciprocal benefit / coordination); the target **reporting** the activity is **exculpatory** (R-NEUTRALITY). Closes the attack of weaponizing our own moat to get a rival flagged.
- **Actors:** member (contributes → earns Signals/Recognition; owns a Distribution), visitor (sees public Signals/Might/Level/Awards), operator (season control, integrity console, level-definition editor, appeals hand-off to M13), automated (all crons).

## 5. The Signals engine — OUTCOME → ATTRIBUTION → AWARD
- **R-OUTCOME (what creates value — qualified outcomes only):** Signals are minted **only** when an eligible **outcome** occurs on a journey that traces (via `rawEvents.anonymousSessionId` + sequence + referrer) back to a contribution. **The event weights are the VALUE of a QUALIFIED outcome-event, NOT an automatic per-action payout** (GPT confirmation FATAL fix): view **0.3** · reaction **1** · reply **1.5** · comment **2** · content-completion **2.5** · **save 3** · **qualified CTA 10** · **conversion 25**. Each event type earns Signal **only after** passing its **qualified-outcome predicate** + a **deduplication window** + the **integrity gate** + a **per-(actor,target) repetition cap**; a bare view/reaction/reply/comment with **no qualified downstream outcome earns ZERO Signal** (it is attribution *path* + analytics, not value — honors INV-4). A **qualified CTA** additionally requires: eligible human · once per (user,target,window) · min dwell on destination handoff · not self-authored · not datacenter/dup · redirect completed · reversible if the network rejects. Every qualified value is then × the actor's **legitimacy** (INV-2).
- **R-ATTRIB (who gets credit — V1 positional):** a qualified outcome's value is split across the contributions **actually on its attributed journey** by a **versioned positional model** (`attributionModelVersion`): **~85%** to the post author, **~15%** across the **eligible commenter pool** — where the commenter pool includes **only comments encountered on the attributed journey or meeting a defined downstream-contribution rule**, and the 15% is divided **by contribution evidence, never evenly across all comments** (GPT confirmation FATAL fix — stops low-value comments harvesting a hit post's credit). Behind an `AttributionEngine` interface so Tier-2 (category-Markov) / Tier-3 (sampled-Shapley) can replace it without touching the ledger. Attribution = **association, not causation**; holdouts/exploration are preserved for later causal testing.
- **R-CONFIDENCE (don't over-trust thin evidence):** each award is damped by a **confidence factor** = `betaBinomialLowerBound(positive, trials) × (1 − exp(−distinctHumans / scale)) × saturating(volume)`. Distinct humans outweigh repeat interactions; volume saturates via `ln`; a **minimum reach floor** applies. Twenty ring accounts at low legitimacy contribute **less** than one genuine high-legitimacy human.
- **R-LEDGER (append-only lifecycle):** each award is a `signalLedger` row: **provisional** at award time (paid at **80%** of computed value) → **finalized** at settlement (`attribution.settle`, ≤7-day window per outcome type) → or **reversed** (outcome refunded/invalidated) → or **clawed_back** (integrity confirmed; **cascades** to downstream awards that depended on it). **Displayed Signals = `max(Σ finalized, 0)`** and are **smoothed** for display (a sudden spike invites scrutiny + imitation = a gaming pipeline; the public number eases in).
- **R-VIEWS (two views, one ledger):** `signalSummary` maintains, per user and per Distribution: **Total** (Σ finalized, lifetime) · **Active** (Σ finalized × `clamp((90 − daysSince)/90, 0, 1)`, the 90-day weighted-decay window) · **Pending** (Σ provisional). **Total** feeds the permanent record + trust capacity; **Active** feeds Might. Public profile shows **Total** as the headline "Signals"; the ladder consumes **Active**.
- **R-GRACE (don't punish honest newcomers):** a new account's and a new (actor→author) pair's **first N interactions** are **exempt** from reciprocity-decay and cluster-damping (a real creator's first supporters are structurally friends = a false "ring"). Grace lifts once volume/velocity crosses a threshold. Peer-prediction / behavior-model damping activates **progressively behind a data-volume gate** (≈0 influence at cold-start; it needs platform history to be fair).

## 6. The moat — hidden legitimacy, the bootstrap paradox
- **R-LEGIT (the score):** `legitimacyScores.value ∈ [0,1]` per actor = the **geometric mean** of bounded components (`legitimacy.component`): account_age · activity_diversity · interaction_diversity · content_quality · temporal_humanity · device_independence · reciprocity_balance. **Geometric mean = one near-zero component tanks the whole score** (you cannot compensate a bot-like signature with volume). Recomputed on a rolling cadence (`legitimacy.recompute`), **snapshotted onto each event at cast time** (`rawEvents`/reaction `weightAtCast`) so a later recompute never retroactively rewrites history mid-window. **But confirmed fraud OVERRIDES the cast-time snapshot** — a clawback/recompute corrects the affected ledger entries (snapshot = reproducibility, clawback = correctness; GPT confirmation).
- **R-BOOTSTRAP (why it's a moat):** to inflate Signals you need high-legitimacy actors; to raise legitimacy you must behave legitimately over time across diverse, independent, well-timed, outcome-producing interactions. **The only way to fake the system is to actually use it honestly.** That is the moat.
- **R-SHADOW (no gradient):** suspected activity is **shadow-damped** — the event still increments the visible counter but contributes a **fraction** of its weight to Signals/Reach/trust. The actor is **never told**. Because there's no "this didn't count" feedback, an attacker cannot hill-climb toward a working exploit.
- **R-GRAPH (detection only, proportional, never ban):** `engagementEdges` (actor→author aggregates) power **batch** coordination detection (dense reciprocal clusters, velocity Z-scores, shared-device/timing signatures) → write `integrityFlags` with a **disposition** (`monitor` / `damp` / `neutralize`). Graph signals **only reduce weight**; they **never** grant influence and **never** auto-ban (human sanction = M13). Confidence-damping compounds with graph-damping.
- **R-NEUTRALITY (Recipient Neutrality — GPT confirmation FATAL fix; INV-11):** unsolicited **inbound** activity can never, by itself, harm its recipient. Manufactured suspicious reactions/joins/clicks/bursts aimed **at** a creator must **never** reduce that creator's Integrity Confidence, finalized legitimate Signals, Reach, or Might. Risk attaches to the **source actors + their coordination cluster**, not the target: suspect inbound events contribute **zero or stay pending**; the target's valid prior outcomes are **unchanged**; distribution may freeze only the **suspect incremental** activity, never the target's whole standing; a **target sanction requires independent evidence of control / solicitation / payment / reciprocal benefit / coordination**; if the target **reported** the activity that is **exculpatory**; and the target's **public Level is unchanged during an inbound-poisoning investigation** unless independent target involvement is established. (Closes the attack of weaponizing our own moat to get a rival flagged/damped.)
- **R-CALIBRATE (pre-launch):** legitimacy is calibrated on historical `rawEvents` **before** launch so the median honest actor sits ≈**0.85** and the bottom ~5% is flagged for review — otherwise day-1 deflation would punish everyone. Calibration constants are config + audit-logged.

## 7. Trust-tier + loop-brakes
- **R-TRUST (the gate):** `TrustTier = min(SignalCapacityTier, IntegrityCeiling, AccountStanding)`. **SignalCapacityTier** = a banded function of **Total Signals** (earned capacity). **IntegrityCeiling** = a cap from legitimacy + open integrity flags (a compromised/ring/traffic-buying account is capped regardless of Signals). **AccountStanding** = a **discrete** standing from verification + moderation state (M7/M13) — **good / limited / suspended**, NOT a third continuous score (GLM clarification). The **minimum** governs — you rise only when all three agree.
- **R-WEIGHT (reaction influence):** a reactor's weight in attribution = `min(3.0, 1 + log10(1 + effectiveSignal) × k)` where `effectiveSignal` is legitimacy-adjusted own-content Signal (= `signalReputation`, **never Recognition**).
- **R-BRAKES (bound the Matthew loop Signal→trust→weight→ranking→Signal):** (1) **log + hard cap** at ~3.0; (2) **no recursive same-window influence** — trust used in a day's attribution is a **snapshot taken at the START of the day** (today's earnings can't raise today's own weight); (3) **rolling recompute** (anti-aristocracy — old Signals decay out of Active); (4) M9 exploration + confidence-damping already blunt amplification; (5) periodic **holdout audit** (do high-trust reactions still predict real outcomes? if not, recalibrate `k`); (6) an **influence budget** caps any single actor's share of another's attributed value per window.

## 8. Recognition → badges → Awards → Podium (backend status, cosmetic)
- **R-RECOG (compute):** `recognition.rollup` derives a windowed, **quality-weighted (not count)** Recognition score per `recognition.role` from local wins (accepted answers, `valuable` comments, reviews, debate outcomes, participation) — personas/staff excluded, legitimacy-weighted, percentile-normalized for Overall. This is the M9 Podium's source (M12 computes, M9 renders): Overall · Commenter · Helper · Reviewer · Creator · Debater · Rising × 24H/7D/1M, min 25 eligible else "Podium is forming" *(updated 2026-08-26, Wave 7C L25 — matches data-model `leaderboard≥25`)*. **Recognition is computed independently from local wins and NEVER reads `signalLedger`** (GLM firewall-reverse check) — the two currencies share no source rows.
- **R-BADGE (mint + validity):** durable achievements mint `badges` rows (`badge.type`): **level_milestone** ("Reached Star · Season 2"), **recognition_role** ("Top Helper · March"), **profile_completion** (from M7), **discoverer** (first to a newly-unlocked level). **Badge HISTORY is append-only; badge VALIDITY has a state** `provisional → finalized → revoked` (GPT confirmation FATAL fix): a revoked badge **disappears from the public Awards count + shelf** but is **retained in the internal audit trail**. **Inactivity or a Level drop NEVER revokes a badge** (the founder's "levels change, badges don't" holds); **only confirmed fraud / impersonation / material calculation error** revokes (via M13).
- **R-AWARDS (the public metric):** **Awards** = a Distribution's/user's public **shelf + count** of earned badges. It is the third public Distribution metric (**Reach · Signals · Awards**). Because every badge is **quality-gated at mint**, the count is not grindable — it reads like a trophy shelf, not a progress bar. **Awards is pure output of Recognition and feeds nothing** (INV-1).
- **R-FIREWALL-EVOLVED:** the essence survives the inversion — *local status buys visibility of status, never distribution*. Recognition/Awards/Podium are cosmetic; Signals/Might are the earned, moat-protected value that actually drives reach. Featuring/curation still **never** consult Recognition rank (operator process rule, carried from DEC-SIGNAL-FIREWALL).

## 9. Distribution + Reach (single-channel, ships now)
- **R-DIST (the channel):** every member may own **one** `distributions` row (`ownershipMode = single` V1). Members join via a **visible, deliberate action** — never auto-joined from following a thread, commenting, or downloading.
- **R-REACH (earned; public count vs internal factor):** the **public Reach = the COUNT of verified/active/integrity-qualified members** ("8,420 members" — a clean, honest integer users can reconcile with their audience; GLM display fix). The **internal `reachFactor` = Σ member.legitimacy** (the legitimacy-weighted "effective reach") is what feeds Might — **never shown as the public number**. The raw all-time historical count is **admin-only**. Leaving a channel is **log-scaled** to a negligible `reachFactor` change (anti-suppression, INV-10); a mass exodus is visible and flaggable.
- **R-PHASE2 (store metrics, skeleton):** when a store is enabled in a Distribution (social-commerce phase), three more public metrics activate — **Sales** (verified completed, non-refunded; revenue only if opted in) · **Review Score** (verified-buyer, legitimacy-weighted; a seller cannot remove legitimate negatives) · **Vouch** (`vouches`: one active vouch per member, revocable, 12-month reconfirm, legitimacy-gated, **not** auto-minted from a purchase). All inherit the moat.
- **R-DN (forward-model, DEFERRED):** `ownershipMode = collaborative` + a role skeleton is reserved for 2–5-owner Distribution Networks; Signals attribute to **contributions/users first**, then roll into the DN by a separate policy. **OPEN:** co-owner Might attribution (equal vs by-contribution) — **DEC-M12-DN-ATTRIB**, decide before building DN.

## 10. Might + the cosmic Level ladder
- **R-MIGHT (the score):** `Might = √(legitimacyWeightedReach × activeSignals)` — **unbounded**, geometric-mean (a huge audience with dead engagement, or big Signals with no reach, both collapse toward 0). This is the number a creator watches grow ("prove your Might"). Recomputed continuously (`might.recompute`); **displayed** via the monthly Level commit (INV-7).
- **R-LEVELS (the ladder — 10 cosmic ranks):** ascending, each a percentile **band** of Might over the eligible pool, with a public identity:

  | # | Level | Identity (how it resonates) | Milestone band |
  |---|-------|------------------------------|----------------|
  | 1 | **Orbit** | You've arrived — Distribution created, circling, on the map. | entry / all eligible |
  | 2 | **Comet** | You're moving — first real momentum, people see the trail. | top 50% |
  | 3 | **Moon** | You have gravity — a steady, returning audience. | top 20% |
  | 4 | **Planet** | You're a world — a community orbits you. | top 10% |
  | 5 | **Star** | You generate light — you draw people by your own energy. | top 3% *(launch apex)* |
  | 6 | **Supernova** | You broke out — a platform-visible surge. | top 1% |
  | 7 | **Pulsar** | You create stars — you seed new creators. | top 0.3% |
  | 8 | **Galaxy** | You contain multitudes — a whole ecosystem. | top 0.1% |
  | 9 | **Universe** | A defining force on the platform. | top 0.03% |
  | 10 | **Multiverse** | Legend — near-mythic apex. | **percentile floor AND absolute top ~100** |

- **R-MILESTONE (dynamic, concrete, rising — with explicit precedence):** each level is a **Might milestone** — a concrete number (e.g. Comet = 1,000 Might) **derived** from its percentile band, **frozen for the Season**, and **recomputed upward** each year (Comet 1,000 → ~10,000 = "the milestone always goes up"). **Precedence when the percentile-derived threshold would jump too far (GPT confirmation FATAL fix — percentile vs 50%-cap can conflict):** `SeasonThreshold = min(percentileDerivedCandidate, priorSeasonThreshold × 1.50)`. When the 50% cap binds, the level is published as a **transition threshold** (explicitly labeled — it does **not** claim the exact percentile that Season) and **converges back to its target percentile over later Seasons**. **Multiverse** is the one deliberate exception to pure-percentile: eligibility = **meets the percentile floor AND sits within the absolute top ~100** eligible Distributions (legendary at any scale).
- **R-SEASON (annual freeze / recalibration):** `signalSeasons` runs **1 year**. Boundary flow: **T-30** announce current + projected thresholds; **T-0** recompute percentile breakpoints from the live Might distribution (`season.recalibrate`) and **freeze** them; **T+1** publish results ("[X] advanced · badges are permanent · your Level is where you stand today in a community N× larger · climb again"). Within a Season, only **your** Might crossing a frozen threshold moves you.
- **R-PROMOTE / R-DEMOTE (asymmetric, humane):** **promotion** is continuous/monthly (sustained ~30 days above the line + ≥2 outcome families + no single contribution dominating + integrity clear → immediate + a permanent milestone badge). **Routine demotion** happens **only at the annual boundary**, **max one level** per recalibration, with a **holdover grace** (within ~10% below the line → 30-day holdover, exact gap shown, no panic countdown). **Integrity corrections** (fraud/compromise/invalid-reach/clawback) may drop a level **immediately** and are **not** "competitive demotion." **Why asymmetric (documented so future engineers never "fix" it into a streak):** promotion is monthly because positive reinforcement should be immediate; routine demotion is annual because rank-decay exists for **accuracy (current standing), not punishment (manufactured urgency)** — monthly demotion would convert a competitive rank into a streak-like compulsion loop (GLM confirmation). **No monthly inactivity penalty exists** (GPT): the monthly commit promotes + stabilizes display; it never demotes for a missed month.
- **R-DOWNGRADE-ETHICS (reconciles DEC-M6-PSYCH "no streaks-with-loss"):** this is **not** a streak. Streak-loss is unethical because it is (a) short-timescale, (b) an arbitrary counter, (c) erased whole on one lapse. This system **fails all three by design** *iff* the timescale stays long (90-day Active window + ≤monthly commits, never daily) **and** the milestone badge is never touched by rank movement. Then it is an **ATP/chess-Elo ranking** (honest current standing; drops if you stop competing; never erases that you held #1). **Framing = accuracy:** every drop reads *"Your Level reflects your current reach — [Peak] remains yours forever in your Journal,"* never "you dropped/fell/lost." The **first** thing a downgraded creator sees is the **permanent badge sitting untouched next to the change** (badge-preservation is the headline, not a footnote). An opt-out hides Level/progress/badges without changing the math.
- **R-REVEAL (progressive):** show the current level, all below, and the **next** milestone (name + requirement + a **two-component** progress read: Reach% / Signal% / sustained-days). Levels **above next** render as a **silhouette with a partial progress fill** (masked-but-present = mystery, not confusion) — never their name or exact threshold. A platform-global "highest Level reached (N creators)" is shown; first-to-reach a newly-unlocked level fires a **community discovery event** + a permanent **discoverer** badge.
- **R-COLDSTART (hybrid → percentile):** below ~**1,000** eligible channels, levels use **fixed Might thresholds** (marked "Founding Season", intentionally accessible, granting no permanent power); at ≥1,000 the pool switches to **percentile** bands; upper levels (**Supernova → Multiverse**) stay **silhouettes** until the pool is large enough to justify them (the cosmic canon *is* the reveal + cold-start + legendary-apex mechanism).
- **R-STABILITY:** hysteresis buffer ~**15%** + sustained-promotion (anti boundary-flicker); a **dormant** channel (Might = 0 for **180 days**) is marked `dormant` and excluded from the pool until it posts again; notifications are **differential and honest** ("your Might declined" vs "the bar rose past you").
- **R-ANTI-SUPPRESSION (verify — INV-10 + INV-11):** percentiles are computed over the **broadest eligible active population** so any single sabotage dilutes to ~0; leaving a rival = negligible log-scaled `reachFactor`; reports/hidden-negatives don't move a rival's legitimacy or Might until human moderation confirms; **Recipient Neutrality** (R-NEUTRALITY) blocks inbound-poisoning. **Structural closer (GLM):** the Signal basket has **NO negative events** — every weight is positive (view 0.3 … conversion 25), so an attacker **cannot subtract** a rival's Signal by any action; they can only *add* (which helps the rival). **Forward-compat constraint (GLM — document now):** IF a negative reaction is ever introduced, it MUST be **legitimacy-gated with an asymmetric threshold** (only legitimacy ≥ a floor may register a negative observation) or the suppression analysis reopens. **Accepted residual risk (GLM + Sonnet): coordinated ENGAGEMENT WITHDRAWAL** — a coalition of genuine users organizing off-platform to *stop* engaging with a rival — is **structurally invisible** to an interaction-graph detector (absence is not an edge). It is slow-acting, dilutes to ~0.04%/rival in a broad pool, and costs more to organize than it returns; **logged as accepted residual** with a **monitoring hook: surface to an operator any rank drop that correlates with NO legitimacy/moderation events** ("an anomaly with no cause").

## 18. Fixtures, Tests & Acceptance Criteria
- **Fixtures:** an honest creator (legit 0.85, growing Might), a ring (5 reciprocal accts legit 0.15), a compromised whale (high Total Signals + open integrity flag), a cold-start pool (<1000), a full-scale pool (percentile), a season-boundary with a would-be 2-level drop, a suppression brigade.
- **AC (Given-When-Then, testable):**
  - **AC-1** Given a ring of 5 reciprocal low-legitimacy accounts, When they mint outcomes for each other, Then attributed Signals sum to **less than one** genuine high-legitimacy human's, and **no** "discounted" feedback is surfaced to them.
  - **AC-2** Given a creator at Comet who becomes inactive 90 days, When Active Signals decay below the frozen Comet threshold at the next monthly commit within-season / at boundary per rules, Then live Level drops per R-DEMOTE **and** the "Reached Comet" badge remains present and unmodified.
  - **AC-3** Given a Season boundary where the eligible pool grew 10×, When `season.recalibrate` runs, Then thresholds rise (≤50%), **no** Distribution drops more than **1** level, and every prior milestone badge is intact.
  - **AC-4** Given Recognition/Awards/Podium values change, When Might/Signals/Reach/trust are recomputed, Then they are **unaffected** (firewall — INV-1).
  - **AC-5** Given a rival brigade leaves a creator's channel and mass-reports them, When Might/Level recompute, Then the creator's legitimacy and Might are **unchanged** until human moderation confirms, and reach change is negligible (log-scaled).
  - **AC-6** Given an outcome is refunded after finalize, When settlement reruns, Then a reversal entry cascades and displayed Signals never go negative.
  - **AC-7** Given a pool < 1,000 eligible, When levels are assigned, Then fixed thresholds apply and Supernova+ render as silhouettes.
  - **AC-8** *(Sonnet — badge-headline literal test)* Given a creator is demoted a Level, When the drop notification renders, Then its **first visible element is the preserved Peak badge** (not the Now-level change), and the copy uses "current reach / the field moved," never "you lost/fell."
  - **AC-9** *(GPT — recipient neutrality)* Given an attacker floods suspicious reactions/joins/clicks at a rival, When integrity + Might recompute, Then the rival's finalized Signals / Reach / Might / Level are **unchanged**, the flags attach to the **source** actors, and the rival's own report of the activity is **exculpatory**.
  - **AC-10** *(GPT — outcome qualification)* Given a bare view/reaction/reply/comment with **no** qualified downstream outcome, When the engine runs, Then it mints **ZERO** Signal (weights are outcome values, not per-action payouts).
  - **AC-11** *(GPT — badge revocability)* Given a milestone badge is later confirmed fraudulent, When it is revoked, Then it **leaves the public Awards count/shelf** but **remains in the audit trail**; and inactivity/level-drop **never** triggers revocation.
  - **AC-12** *(GPT — threshold precedence)* Given a percentile-derived Season threshold would exceed prior × 1.5, When `season.recalibrate` runs, Then the frozen threshold = `min(percentile candidate, prior × 1.5)` and the level is published as a **transition threshold**, converging toward its target percentile in later Seasons.
